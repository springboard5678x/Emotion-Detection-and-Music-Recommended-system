About MoodMate

🎵 What is MoodMate?

MoodMate is an AI-powered music discovery platform that analyzes your emotions and recommends the perfect soundtrack for your mood. Using advanced natural language processing and machine learning, we understand how you feel and curate personalized playlists just for you.

🤖 How It Works

  1. Tell us how you're feeling in natural language
  2. Our AI model analyzes your emotional state
  3. We map your emotion to music sentiments
  4. Get personalized song recommendations instantly

🔒 Privacy

Your mood data is processed locally and stored temporarily only to provide recommendations. We respect your privacy and don't share your emotional data with third parties.
